Do not copy IRLP.RefConnMon.dll.config over your settings if you already set one up.

Upgrading from 1.2 or earlier: this version runs on .NET 10 (install the .NET 10 Runtime: https://dotnet.microsoft.com/en-us/download/dotnet/10.0).
The settings file is now named IRLP.RefConnMon.dll.config instead of IRLP.RefConnMon.exe.config.
Copy your values from the old .exe.config into the new .dll.config (the keys are unchanged).
Your existing reflector .txt files still work.
